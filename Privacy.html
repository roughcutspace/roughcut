Privacy Policy

Roughcut Space Limited

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Roughcut Space creates private digital journals that give young people somewhere to record their experiences, memories, achievements and everything in between. Our journals are designed to keep personal information personal, without user accounts, social media features or unnecessary data collection.

What information do we collect?

Our journals do not require users to create an account or provide personal information. Journal entries, photographs, goals and other content are stored on the user's device, not on Roughcut Space servers. Roughcut Space does not receive copies of this content and cannot access, read or retrieve it.

We do not include advertising, third-party tracking or usage analytics in our journals, and we do not collect information about how people use them.

Photos and microphone access

Our journals may request permission to access photographs and use the device's microphone. These permissions allow users to add their own photos and dictate journal entries rather than typing them. These features are optional and are only used when the user chooses to use them.

On Android, speech-to-text processing is designed to take place directly on the device. On Apple devices, dictation is expected to use Apple's built-in speech recognition, which may process speech on the device or through Apple services depending on the device and settings. Roughcut Space does not receive or store audio recordings or dictated speech. Users can manage photo and microphone permissions through their device settings.

Storage, backups and deletion

Journal content is stored locally on the user's device. Where backup and export features are available, users can save copies of their journals for safekeeping or transfer to another device. Roughcut Space does not receive or store these files. Users choose where exported files and backups are saved and who, if anyone, they share them with.

Backups may be encrypted and protected by a password. Users are responsible for keeping backup files and passwords safe, as Roughcut Space cannot recover forgotten passwords or retrieve journals from lost or damaged devices. Deleting journal content or uninstalling the app may permanently remove information stored on the device. Any separately saved backups or exports will need to be managed or deleted separately by the user.

Purchases

Our journals may be purchased through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Payments are handled by the relevant app store under its own privacy policy. Roughcut Space does not collect or store payment card details through its journals.

Our website and enquiries

Our website provides information about Roughcut Space and its digital journals. If someone contacts us by email, we receive their email address and whatever information they choose to include in their message. We use that information to respond to the enquiry and handle any related follow-up, not to send advertising or marketing. Our website hosting and email providers may process technical or contact information needed to deliver their services.

Children's privacy

Our journals are designed with young people in mind. They do not include public profiles, social feeds or messaging between users, and personal journal content is not shared with Roughcut Space. Parents and caregivers are encouraged to help younger users understand the importance of keeping their devices, backups and personal information safe.

Your information

Roughcut Space does not hold copies of users' journals or the personal content they contain. Users can edit or delete their own journal content directly within the app. If someone contacts us, they can request access to or correction of personal information we hold from that correspondence, and ask us to delete it where appropriate, subject to applicable legal obligations.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our products and services develop. The current version will be available on our website with the date of the most recent update.
