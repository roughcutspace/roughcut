Being a parent and a teacher means spending a lot of time around young people as they tackle challenges, chase goals, celebrate achievements and pick themselves up when things don't go to plan.

Roughcut Space grew from a simple idea. Give young people somewhere personal to keep those moments. Something they'll enjoy using now, and appreciate looking back on later.

Your memories. Your business.

Roughcut journals are yours, and yours alone. No likes. No followers. No pressure. Your words, photos and memories stay on your device. We don't collect them, read them or store them on our servers.

What you put in your journal isn't our business. And we intend to keep it that way.

Not much of a writer? No problem.

Not everyone loves writing. That's why we've made speech to text an essential part of our journals. Just talk, in your own words, at your own pace. Or type if you'd rather. It's a journal, not homework.
